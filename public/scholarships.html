STHP and talent hunt programmes

STSP and scholarship tests

Review the published syllabus, practise timed questions, and keep a record of weak topics. Never rely on unofficial posts for deadlines or eligibility decisions.

APPLICATION CHECKLIST

Prepare before the deadline

Academic documents

Keep transcripts, certificates, student ID details, and required photographs ready. Follow the exact format requested by the institution.

Eligibility and dates

Check age, domicile, district, marks, degree level, application dates, and test venue requirements in the latest official notice.

Test practice

Practise under timed conditions, review mistakes, and revisit the official syllabus regularly. Do not assume sample questions will appear in the actual exam.

How to verify an opportunity

  1. Find the latest announcement on the official institution or programme website.
  2. Check the publication date and application deadline.
  3. Confirm eligibility and whether the opportunity covers tuition, accommodation, stipend, travel, or other costs.
  4. Use only official application portals and contact details.
  5. Never pay an unverified agent or share passwords, OTPs, or bank credentials.
