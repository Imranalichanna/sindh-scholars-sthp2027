POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Draft policy — review and update before launch.

Sindh Scholars Academy collects only information needed to provide student accounts, record test attempts, protect the service, and administer learning resources. Depending on features enabled, this may include display name, account email managed by the authentication provider, student ID, optional district or education level, test answers, scores, and timestamps.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate students, save attempts, display personal results according to the test's publication policy, prevent abuse, and administer the platform. We do not intentionally publish private answers, email addresses, or phone numbers in public result lists.

Access and retention

Access should be limited by role and database policies. Account holders should contact the academy using its configured contact channel to request correction or deletion. Retention periods, backups, and applicable legal requirements must be defined by the operator before public launch.

Third-party services

The platform may use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and Cloudflare for hosting. Review those providers' current privacy terms and configure services appropriately.

Contact

Add a monitored academy contact address before publishing this policy. This draft is not legal advice and must be reviewed for the jurisdictions and students served.
