ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Opportunity should be accessible.

Sindh Scholars Academy is a free learning initiative to help students discover education opportunities and prepare with confidence. We aim to make practice resources and scholarship information easier to access, wherever a student lives.

We are an independent educational initiative and do not claim official affiliation with any university or examination body unless explicitly verified.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is preparation free?

Yes. The academy's preparation resources are intended to be free. Any third-party fees, where relevant, should be checked with the provider.

Can I see my result immediately?

Result access depends on the policy set for each test. Some results may remain private until an administrator releases them.

Is the academy an official university programme?

No official affiliation is claimed by this website unless it is explicitly verified and stated.

How can I report a problem?

Use the contact details configured by the academy administrators or the community channel.
